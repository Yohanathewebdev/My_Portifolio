# ADR-010: First-party analytics

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** SDD v3.0

## Context

Owner-facing analytics are useful for public portfolios, but visitor tracking
must respect the privacy and consent requirements in §§23 and 30. The product
also needs dashboards that remain fast as traffic grows; querying raw events
for every chart would make latency and retention costs unpredictable.

## Decision

Collect cookieless, first-party page-view events with privacy-preserving
coarse metadata, then roll them into time-bucketed aggregate tables. Analytics
are served by the platform and scoped to the portfolio. Retention and
aggregation jobs run through the `analytics` queue, and owner dashboards read
rollups rather than scanning raw events.

## Alternatives considered

- **Third-party analytics:** rejected because it adds a data processor,
  cross-origin tracking, consent complexity, and a weaker ownership story.
- **Cookies or fingerprinting:** rejected because they are unnecessary for
  portfolio-level aggregate views and create avoidable privacy risk.
- **Raw-event dashboard queries:** rejected because volume would make query
  latency and cost grow with traffic.

## Consequences

The platform must build ingestion, aggregation, retention, and dashboard
queries itself. Individual visitor journeys and fine-grained attribution are
not product guarantees. In exchange, analytics remain first-party, reduce
consent burden, keep owner dashboards predictable at volume, and support
portfolio-scoped deletion and retention policies.
